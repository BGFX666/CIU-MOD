A Tool That points to emhance your Chicken Invaders Universe game experience.
Made with C++. Build.bat can be a sample(im so lazy to change the bat to auto adjust to everyones device).

#MainFeatures
1.Invincible(when you collide with a chicken, you wont die,instead,the chicken will die);
2.No Weapon Heat(some weapon may still have heat);
3.controllable damage boost(you can do it by modifing source);
4.LightUpButton(Randomly light up a enemy while the button was pressed);
*5.this tool just suitable for version(1600300).Before Using,ensure that game lib size(should be 24.00M(25161496bit)) to prevent unexpected crash.
